Privacy policy

Ore & Tar social media platform · Last updated 28 September 2026

Who we are

Ore and Tar Enterprises (Pvt) Ltd, Harare, Zimbabwe ("Ore & Tar", "we") operates this platform (the "Service") to plan, publish and measure social media content for its own brands and for its clients. Ore & Tar is the data controller for the personal data described here. Contact: manenji@oreandtar.com.

What we collect

  • People who use the Service: name and email address, received from Google when you sign in with your Google account (we hold no passwords), and a record of the actions each person takes.
  • Connected social accounts: when someone with the right to do so connects a Facebook Page, an Instagram professional account or a LinkedIn Page, we receive an access token, the account's identifier and name, and the permissions granted. For LinkedIn we also receive the connecting member's identifier.
  • Content: posts, images, video and audio that teams upload or create in the Service, including drafts and media produced with AI, and the identifiers the platform assigns when we publish them. Uploaded files are scanned for malware on our own infrastructure.
  • AI assistant records: the instructions given to the Service's AI assistants and the steps they took, kept so a team can see how a draft was produced.
  • Performance data: aggregate statistics the platforms provide for published posts and connected accounts, such as views, reach, clicks, reactions, follower gains and engagement.
  • Comments on our posts: the text of comments left on posts published through the Service, the commenter's name or handle as the platform shows it (on LinkedIn, the member identifier), the platform's identifier for the comment, and a keyed, one-way hash of the name or handle used to group a person's comments.
  • Link clicks: when someone follows a tracked link in a post, we record the time, the link and a keyed, one-way hash derived from their IP address, browser user agent and the day. We do not store the IP address or user agent.

How we use it

We use this data only to provide the Service: to publish content at the time a brand chooses, to report how it performed, to show a brand's team the comments its posts receive, and to group those comments by theme and sentiment so the brand can understand its audience's feedback. That grouping uses AI models, described below. We do not sell personal data, use it for advertising, or combine one client's data with another's.

AI processing

Drafting, comment classification and media generation use AI models reached through OpenRouter. Every request tells OpenRouter to exclude model providers that collect or train on the data they receive. A brand can also restrict which providers generate its media, and require providers that retain nothing. Comment text sent for classification carries no name, handle or identifier of the commenter.

Who processes it for us

  • Railway (Railway Corporation, United States): hosting, databases and file storage, in the United States.
  • Google: sign-in to the Service.
  • OpenRouter (United States) and the model providers it routes to: AI processing, as described above.
  • Meta and LinkedIn receive the content we publish on your instruction, under their own terms and privacy policies.

Because the Service is hosted in the United States, personal data is transferred there from Zimbabwe and other countries.

How we protect it

Access tokens are encrypted at rest, each with its own data key, and are never shown to users or written to logs. Access within the Service is limited by role and by brand. Our logs record identifiers and error codes, not content or tokens.

How long we keep it

  • An access token is destroyed when the account is disconnected in the Service, when it is replaced by a new token, or when the brand is deleted.
  • Other data about a brand, including content, AI assistant records, performance data, comments and link clicks, is kept while the brand uses the Service. It is deleted when the brand or the client's account is deleted, or earlier on request.
  • Database backups expire at the end of their retention window. If one is restored before then, pending deletions are applied again.
  • After a deletion we keep the record of who did what (the audit log) and evidence of what was approved for publication. These contain identifiers, not the published content.

Content already published stays on the platform until it is deleted. On the brand's instruction we can edit the text of a published post or delete it from Facebook, LinkedIn and X; the platform itself is asked to do so and confirms it. Instagram does not allow this: Instagram posts must be deleted on Instagram by the account's owner.

Your choices and rights

You can remove the Service's access to your social accounts at any time, in the Service or in the platform's own settings. You can ask us for a copy of your personal data, to correct it, or to delete it. How to do that is set out in our data deletion instructions. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you may complain to the data protection authority where you live; in Zimbabwe, that is the Postal and Telecommunications Regulatory Authority of Zimbabwe (POTRAZ).
